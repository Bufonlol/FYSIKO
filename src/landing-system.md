# FYSIKO landing design system

- Audience: people in Orizaba with pain, reduced mobility or sports injuries.
- Primary task: understand whether FYSIKO can help and start a WhatsApp valuation.
- Thesis: a local sports clinic that feels direct, capable and human.
- Palette: ink `#071827`, blue `#087ff5`, cyan `#20a4ff`, white `#ffffff`, mist `#f3f7fa`.
- Type: condensed display headings with a neutral sans-serif body.
- Shape: restrained 12–18px radii, squared image cards, circular icon controls.
- Breakpoints: 720px mobile, 1024px tablet, 1280px content max.
- Motion: short reveals and hover lift; disabled with reduced-motion.
